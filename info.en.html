Aerial images of Berlin from almost 100 years, from 1928 until today, from the Geoportal Berlin. Choose a year at the top and see how the city has changed. Split the screen to compare two years – all panes move and zoom together, and the strip with the years changes the image of the pane you clicked last.

The images from 1928 and 1953 show Berlin before and after the war, since 2002 there is a new image almost every year. For example, you can see how the Hauptbahnhof was built, how Tempelhof Airport became a park or what your neighbourhood looked like almost a hundred years ago.

A project of the OK Lab Berlin. The tiles are served by tiles.codefor.de and the source code is on GitHub: luftbilder.berlin.codefor.de and @jochenklar/maps. More maps are on maps.berlin.codefor.de.
